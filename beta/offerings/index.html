Portals of transformation

This is shadow work.

We work with individuals and couples to heal, awaken, manifest, and live out their destiny.

Transformational guidance Programs

Walking through the portals of our transformation was messy. We didn’t have strong guides, and the shame of infidelity in our culture made it difficult to find them. Through the failures of our healing journey, where we spent a lot of time, money, and energy attempting to heal in “traditional” ways, we discovered that authentic healing isn’t about changing behavior. It’s about the integration of those aspects of our self that are unloved, unwitnessed, forgotten, or even undiscovered.

Behavior is a symptom of an underlying false, limiting, or unhealthy belief system. It is not the issue itself.

A healed person is full of self-love, not in an egoic or self-centered way, but because of an authentic gnosis of who they truly are. When you love yourself, you love others. This has incredible benefits for all of your relationships, and your undesired behaviors automatically and effortlessly fall away.
