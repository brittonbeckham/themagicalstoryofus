Dear seeker, have we got a tale for you

A story of love, sovereignty, sexuality, and soul.

Hi there, we’re Britton and Carolee Beckham. We were born as sixth-generation Mormons and raised up in this high-demand religion.

Married in this paradigm, infidelity threatened our family and marriage. As we used the process of the church, we found that it miserably failed to help us heal. Yet, this was the necessary catalyst that propelled us to leave the church and find our own healing and spiritual connection.

Leaving the church took us on a five-year-long healing journey that led us into places we never could have imagined.

We shed layers of religious conditioning, focused on the inner work of healing emotionally and spiritually while rebuilding our entire relationship from scratch, not to mention integrating all these changes into our family.

And we’ve exited the other side as an embodied and awakened couple with a powerful message to share.

Chapter one · 2017

A look into the past feels like a past life

The year 2017 paints a very different picture of us, but it’s where we were. Not only was there no magic in our life, we didn’t know the possibility even existed.

We simply lacked the understanding of how to free ourselves from this paradigm. It was the promised “dream life” we inherited from our upbringing, the path we were told would bring us the greatest joy.

But it wasn’t actually the life of our choosing, and it wasn’t the life we came here to live.

How things looked back then

  • Emotional disconnection
  • Lackluster sex life
  • Totally codependent
  • Infidelity and betrayal trauma
  • Unsupportive religious structure
  • Lack of spiritual connection
  • Lack of emotional intelligence
  • Striving and clinging
  • Hoping and looping
To experience the magic of life, we must be willing to make room.
Chapter two · The work

We chose to heal. We chose to face our demons.

Not just for the sake of our relationship, but for our individual selves. Once we got a sense of the potential of change, it became clear there was another reality awaiting us, if we worked for it.

As we began to heal, we quickly discovered the magic that awaits those who choose the path of healing: the path of the inner dimensions, of finding your authentic expression and awakening to your true potential.

We gave up everything to find that new reality together, and the ride was an amazing, magical journey.

Oh, and we did it with four children.

What we did to find our healing

  • Left the Mormon church
  • Committed to deeper spirituality
  • Emotional intelligence re-education
  • Committed to healing everything
  • Left a 22-year tech career
  • Committed to saying “yes!”
  • Rebooted our relationship
  • Sexual re-education
  • Sold everything
  • Traveled the world for 18 months
Life was calling us in a new direction, and we went all in. We awakened together.
Chapter three · Now

Now we live the life we only dreamed was possible

Our relationship is rebuilt, and we’ve completely reimagined our life together.

We’re living our truth out loud and guiding others into the freedom that called to us.

We’ve discovered our path for awakening, and developed a unique process for building the life you’ve never imagined you could have.

We’re here to share our journey, inspire, and lead others into their destiny timelines.

What things look like now

  • Healed infidelity and re-established trust
  • Deep emotional connection
  • Hot and spicy sex life
  • Embodied and joyful living
  • Giving our children soul education
  • Magical manifestations
  • Ritual and connection to the Divine

  • Building soul offerings together
  • Authoring tell-all books

We’re here to help guide

Ready to step deeper into your own story?

Having walked this path together for some time now, we’ve developed a process that is effective at getting to the root of our patterns and blocks, with the assistance of what we call “the cosmic clock.”

We say “opportunities” because the work is up to you, of course. You are the true healer, and we’re here to help awaken that healer in you. The path of healing is the path of awakening.
