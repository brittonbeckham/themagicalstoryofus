Dear seeker, have we got a tale for you

A story of love, sovereignty, sexuality, and soul.

Hi there, we’re Britton and Carolee Beckham. We were born as sixth-generation Mormons and raised up in this high-demand religion.

Married inside that paradigm, infidelity threatened our family and our marriage. We turned to the church for help, and it failed to heal us. That failure became the catalyst that propelled us to leave, and to find our own healing and spiritual connection.

What followed was a five-year journey into places we never could have imagined.

I · Then II · The work III · Now Your story
Chapter one · 2017

A look into the past feels like a past life

The year 2017 paints a very different picture of us, but it’s where we were. We were living the promised “dream life” we inherited from our upbringing, the path we were told would bring us the greatest joy. Not only was there no magic in our life, we didn’t know the possibility even existed.

Underneath it, we were emotionally disconnected and totally codependent. Our sex life was lackluster. We had a religious structure that couldn’t support us, and little real spiritual connection or emotional intelligence to fall back on.

When infidelity and betrayal broke it all open, we did what we knew. We kept striving and clinging, hoping and looping.

We simply lacked the understanding of how to free ourselves. It wasn’t the life of our choosing, and it wasn’t the life we came here to live.

To experience the magic of life, we must be willing to make room.
Chapter two · The work

We chose to heal. We chose to face our demons.

Not just for the sake of our relationship, but for our individual selves. Once we got a sense of the potential of change, it became clear there was another reality awaiting us, if we worked for it. So we committed to saying yes.

  1. Left the Mormon churchand committed to a deeper spirituality of our own
  2. Committed to healing everythingincluding re-learning emotional intelligence
  3. Rebooted our relationshipdown to a sexual re-education
  4. Left a 22-year tech career
  5. Sold everything
  6. Traveled the world for 18 monthswith all four children

We gave up everything to find that new reality together, and the ride was an amazing, magical journey.

Life was calling us in a new direction, and we went all in. We awakened together.
Chapter three · Now

Now we live the life we only dreamed was possible

Our relationship is rebuilt, and we’ve completely reimagined our life together.

We’re living our truth out loud and guiding others into the freedom that called to us.

We’ve discovered our path for awakening, and developed a unique process for building the life you’ve never imagined you could have.

Today that looks like a dream home we manifested, soul education for our children, offerings we build together, and the tell-all books we’re writing. We’re here to share our journey, inspire, and lead others into their destiny timelines.
