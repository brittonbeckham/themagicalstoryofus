The Magical Story of Us

Our journey is our message, and we’re here to guide you to unlocking your magical life.

We were born sixth-generation Mormons, raised inside a high-demand religion and married inside that same paradigm. Infidelity threatened our family and became the catalyst that changed everything. The church process failed to heal us, so we left — and spent five years rebuilding our marriage, our spirituality, and our lives from the ground up. We emerged as an embodied, awakened couple with a message to share.

Then

A life we inherited, not one we chose.

2017 looks like a past life now. We were living the promised dream of our upbringing — without knowing another possibility existed, and without knowing how to leave. It was disconnection, a church that could not heal us, betrayal, and no magic.

“To experience the magic of life, we must be willing to make room.”

The Work

We chose to heal — for the marriage, and for ourselves.

Once we felt the potential of change, we gave up everything we knew and followed the inner work all the way through. We left the church, rebuilt the marriage, sold everything, and spent eighteen months on the road. The ride was a magical journey, and we did it with four children.

Now

The life we only dreamed was possible.

Our relationship is rebuilt, and we have completely reimagined our life together. We live our truth out loud.

Now we guide others into the freedom that called to us.
