Demos

A little magic.

Eight subtle effects to choose from. Each band shows one effect on its own. Pick the ones you like, and say where you’d want them.

01 · Drifting threads

To experience the magic of life, we must be willing to make room.

Three fine gold lines flowing slowly sideways at different speeds. My pick for the Our Story quote bands.

02 · Orbits

Life was calling us in a new direction, and we went all in.

Faint orbit rings turning very slowly, with one star travelling along the outer ring. A good fit for the astrology page.

03 · Constellation

Your destiny is written in the stars.

A gold line draws itself between star points once, when the band scrolls into view, then the stars keep twinkling. Scroll away and back to see it again after a refresh.

04 · Starfield

There is a magical story in everyone.

Sparse, barely visible stars twinkling at random. Could sit behind any black section, or the footer.

05 · Infinity trace

The path of healing is the path of awakening.

A point of light travelling a figure-eight loop, echoing the infinity in your sigil. Shown on the maroon band, where it could sit behind the book.

06 · Gold shimmer

Awaken to your cosmic blueprint

A slow sweep of light across the gold italic words in headings, every few seconds. Works on any dark section.

07 · Grain texture

Healing is where the magic is.

A still film-grain texture so the black reads as rich and photographic, not flat. No movement. Subtle on purpose, so look closely.

08 · Cursor glow

We awakened together.

Move your mouse over this band. A soft gold glow follows it. Desktop only, for the dark heroes on Home and Astrology.

On light · Drifting threads

We want to yell from housetops that there is a magical story awaiting each one of us.

The same threads in deep gold on a light section, to show they work on either background.

Layered · Stars, threads, grain, and glow

A story of love and soul

Several effects together, to show the upper limit. I’d use one or two per section, not all of them.
