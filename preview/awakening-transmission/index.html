Full Moon in Gemini · Uranus

Something in you is ready to wake up

This full moon lands at 2 degrees of Gemini, just over a degree from Uranus. Gemini is the sign of the mind, of messages, of the stories we tell ourselves and each other. And Uranus is the one who wakes us up. When it sits this close to the full moon, the light can come on all at once.

Insights may arrive like lightning, the kind that change how you see your life in a single moment. I find that the real magic is in what we do with them next.

Mars & Jupiter in Leo

Courage of the heart

Mars and Jupiter met in Leo last week, and they are still close together, side by side in the sign of the heart. Mars brings the courage to act. Jupiter makes everything bigger. Leo asks us to be seen.

There are mixed feelings out there about this transit, and I understand why. This much fire can feel like a lot. But I see an invitation in it: to give what’s waking up in you a voice, and to follow it with your whole heart.

Full moon ritual
