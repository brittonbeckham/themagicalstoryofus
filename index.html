In one phrase, we're here to help you heal. Healing is where all the magic awaits you. You don't get it AFTER healing, it comes along WITH healing.

So, "what healing do I need" you may ask? Firstly, all humans need it, and no one is "broken". Broken would imply an incapacity to heal, that simply isn't true. Instead, see it from the perspective that everyone's healing needs are different.

Carolee and I use an very individualized and precise approach to help you direct your healing path into exactly what life is calling you to heal, right now!

We've developed an approach we call Ritual Shadow Work (book to debut Fall of 2025). Since we all need healing, and it's actually an crucial part of the reason we have come to planet Earth, why not find the most effective path of this trajectory for you?

How we healing—that is, how fast and how far we move through it—is fully up to our commitment to it. Thus, healing is truly an act of free will. It is a function of our personal commitment.

The presence and intention we bring to the table for our healing is equally matched back by life with its magical gifts!

Our story is an inspirational example of how a commitment to healing will yield an incredibly abundant and fulfilling life.
